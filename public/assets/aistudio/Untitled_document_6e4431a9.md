\#\# 3\. Composable Architecture: How Spenta Apps Mix & Work Together

To match \[Odoo's\](https\://github.com/odoo/odoo) \*"standalone or seamlessly integrated ERP"\* guarantee across AI, ERP, Fintech, and XR workloads, model the website—and the underlying platform story—on a \*\*Core Kernel \+ Manifest Addon\*\* architecture derived directly from Spenta's \*\*L1–L6 Sovereign Fusion Stack\*\* (\[Spenta\_Expanded\_V2\](https\://docs.google.com/presentation/d/183nQzgjjeLYkIux4djJorI5OTZUsMN7d8Yg4mDrvxl8/edit?usp=drivesdk\&ouid=117677985043681236121), Slide 19):

\#\#\# A. The Shared Foundation (\`spenta\_core\` Kernel)

Every app selected in the App Vault automatically inherits five standardized core services so modules never need custom glue code:

* \*\*Identity & Multi-Tenant RBAC:\*\* Unified SSO, role permissions, and branch/site scoping across MIS, Healthcare ERP, Fintech, and BI.  
* \*\*Event & Data Bus (PostgreSQL \+ Kafka \+ Vector DB):\*\* Standardized entity schemas and event streams so an extracted form from \*Document Intelligence (ICR)\* immediately triggers an approval in \*Workflow Automation\* and updates \*Conversational BI\*.  
* \*\*OPA Governance & 0.88 HITL Gate:\*\* Every AI prediction passes an Open Policy Agent (OPA) circuit-breaker; confidence scores \`\< 0.88\` automatically route to a shared Human-in-the-Loop review queue with immutable audit trails.  
* \*\*Sovereign Inference Router (L3):\*\* Abstracts local open-weight models (LLaMA 3.2 Vision, LayoutLMv3, Whisper, quantized Green AI models) across \*\*Managed SaaS\*\*, \*\*Private VPC\*\*, or \*\*On-Prem BYOC\*\* without changing app code.  
* \*\*Channel & Spatial Bridge (L4/L6):\*\* Exposes the same backend state to React web UIs, WhatsApp Business API, Voice pipelines, and Unreal Engine 5 / WebAR viewports.
